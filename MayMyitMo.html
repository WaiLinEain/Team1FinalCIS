Benefits of wearing thanaka

Thanaka can make our skin feel cool and fresh, especially on hot and sunny days. It can also help reduce oiliness and make the skin feel more comfortable. Thanaka may also give some protection from the sun.

Another benefit is that thanaka is a natural product made from the thanaka tree. Many people in Myanmar have used it for a long time. Wearing thanaka is also a good way to keep the beautiful Myanmar tradition alive.
